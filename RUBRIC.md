# NKU Community Hackathon — Rubric

Theme: **Community**

Include **evidence** via observations, interviews, and similar research. Claims without evidence will score lower on users, problem, and desirability.

## Challenges

### Challenge 1 — Help improve student life at NKU

Make the student experience better.

### Challenge 2 — Connect NKU with the community

Show how the solution links campus and the surrounding community (not only students helping students).

## Scoring (100 pts)

| Category | Points | What judges are looking for |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Target users | 15 | Who you are building for. Named users / personas, not “everyone at NKU.” |
| Problem & evidence | 15 | A clear problem backed by observations, interviews, or other evidence. |
| Solution & desirability | 15 | Why this solution fits those users and why they would use it. |
| How (feasibility) | 15 | How it works technically and what is actually built vs mocked. |
| Viability beyond hackathon | 10 | Who owns it after the weekend, how it stays alive, why it lasts. |
| Impact on community | 10 | Effect on NKU student life **and** connection to the wider community. |
| Progress / what you learned | 10 | What the team learned while building and researching. |
| Design & delivery on presentation | 10 | Clarity of the pitch and quality of the demo / design. |

**Total: 100**
